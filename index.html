Research

Course project paper · IMADA, University of Southern Denmark

GRPO for Control

Asbjørn Vesterskov Olsen, Lucas Bergholdt Hansen, Mathias Twisttmann Pedersen

GRPO trains language models without a learned critic by comparing samples within a group. We wanted to see if the same idea works for continuous control, where there is no obvious group to compare against. We ended up with VC-GPG, which mixes the group-relative advantage with an ordinary GAE estimate from a critic.

  • Tested against PPO, SAC and TD3 on CarRacing, cartpole swing-up and acrobot swing-up, with five seeds each.
  • About as good as PPO or slightly better. On CarRacing it ended at 530 against PPO's 458.
  • Clearly behind SAC and TD3 for the same number of training steps.

Bachelor thesis · Spring 2026 · Supervisor: Peter Schneider Kamp

LLM Evaluation & Agentic Post-Training for Tool-Oriented Tasks

Asbjørn Vesterskov Olsen, Mathias Twisttmann Pedersen

We tried to teach Gemma 3 270M, a very small open model, to call tools in Danish. We generated the training data synthetically and fine-tuned on it.

  • More data did not help on its own. A smaller, curated and balanced dataset did.
  • The biggest jump came from a training fix: sample packing had turned off completion-only loss.
  • Single-tool calls work well. No-tool and multi-tool cases only partly.
  • The model scored about the same on an English validation set.
